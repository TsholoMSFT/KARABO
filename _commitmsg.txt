feat(finops): Phase 8 — Cost-optimisation AI panel

Adds an on-demand FinOps analyser that reviews the portfolio's run-cost
estimates and surfaces ranked savings opportunities.

src/lib/cost-optimization-service.ts
  • analyzeCostOptimizations(useCases) — sends a compact JSON snapshot
    (per-use-case monthly/annual USD, assumptions, region, stack) to
    callAIForTask('cost-optimization', …) which routes to GPT-4o-mini
  • Strict JSON schema parsed back into CostOptimization[]: category
    (sku-swap | rightsize | reservation | serverless-shift | caching |
    storage-tier | consolidation | other), title, rationale, affected
    use cases, monthly/annual USD saving, effort, confidence, risk notes
  • System prompt anchors the model to realistic ratios (reservations
    30–55%, rightsize 20–40%, serverless 30–60% for spiky workloads)
    and forbids hallucinating workloads
  • Tolerates accidental ```json fences in the model output

src/components/CostOptimizationPanel.tsx
  • One-click "Analyse" button with spinner; "Re-analyse" once a report
    exists
  • Hero strip showing total $/yr saving in green
  • Ranked list of recommendations: title + category badge + effort +
    confidence chips, rationale, $/yr (right-aligned, bold green),
    optional $/mo subscript, applies-to use case names, optional risk
    notes (amber warning row)
  • Disabled state explains "estimate run cost on at least one use
    case" when nothing eligible

src/components/FinancialImpactTab.tsx
  • Mounted CostOptimizationPanel directly under the Financial Impact
    Overview card, before the per-use-case grid
